AI USE DISCLOSURE
=================

MY OWN WORK
-----------
- The idea of the app is my own: reading the emotions in song lyrics, matching people to a music
  aesthetic, and growing a personal "aura" of colour from every song they add.
- All the product and design decisions are my own, for example: the This-or-That survey, the result
  cards, the aura and its overall colour, starting a new aura when your music changes, the colours
  over time, exporting the lyrics stats, no login (privacy first), and keeping the prototype
  focused on one person (I removed the friend and share-code features).
- I wrote the name and tagline of the Rainbow Sunshine aesthetic.

WHERE THE LOGIC IS
------------------
All of the program's logic is in Python: main.py, analyser.py, compatibility.py, archive.py,
models.py and storage.py. This includes reading the lyrics, scoring the 14 emotions, matching the
8 aesthetics (cosine similarity), recommending songs, saving songs and auras, working out each
aura and its colour, and writing the stats export. The web page only displays what Python sends.

WHAT AN AI ASSISTANT MADE (Claude, by Anthropic)
------------------------------------------------
- The user interface was AI-generated: everything in static/ (HTML, CSS and JavaScript),
  following my ideas and feedback.
- The Python code and the unit tests (tests/) were also written with the AI assistant, from my
  ideas and instructions. I reviewed them and typed parts of models.py and storage.py myself.
- Data drafted with the AI assistant: lexicon.json, modifiers.json and aesthetics.json (except
  Rainbow Sunshine's name and tagline). The lexicon was expanded with words selected from the
  NRC Emotion Intensity Lexicon and cross-checked against the NRC VAD Lexicon; every word's
  source is listed in docs/lexicon_sources.csv. No NRC files are included.
- The mood profiles of the songs in songs.json are AI estimates. The song list itself is the
  Billboard Year-End Hot 100 of 2025.
